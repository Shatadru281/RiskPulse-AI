## Hygiene and honest claims

- Dependencies, builds, runtime state, secrets and recordings stay outside Git.
- Demo mode uses committed fixtures. Optional live-feed responses change over time and are not required to reproduce the demonstration.
- No confidential client data is included. Synthetic reports are labeled.
- AI assistance is disclosed. The candidate must understand and explain the work.
- Actual incremental commits are preserved; development history must not be fabricated or backdated.
- No measured accuracy, financial calibration or efficiency gain over another system is claimed.
- The guideline's support email is a placeholder, not a verified contact.
